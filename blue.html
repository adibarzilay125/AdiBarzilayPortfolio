Pina - Shen

A short animated sequence created for the music video Pina by the band Shen.
The closing sequence of the music video follows a figure running toward the moon, until they eventually merge and become one. I wanted this final moment to create a sense of closure, bringing the character, the moon, and the universe together as one.
