Elkosh

The sequence combines archival footage with new footage shot by me. Through editing, pacing, and sound, I created a fictional documentary-style story that connects the original material with the present-day location.
The project focuses on how existing footage can be edited and reworked to create a new narrative and atmosphere. The murder case presented in the video is entirely fictional and is not based on real events.
